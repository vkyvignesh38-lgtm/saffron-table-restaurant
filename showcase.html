Saffron Table, desktop & mobile

A six-page responsive website for a fictional South Indian restaurant in Chennai. Both previews below are the live, working site, so you can scroll, filter the menu, open the gallery and try the reservation form.

Palette

Warm charcoal and ivory with a single gold accent. Banana-leaf green and chilli red appear only in the veg / non-veg marks.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond for headings and prices, Manrope for body text, labels and buttons.

Details

Kolam-inspired logo mark, FSSAI-style diet symbols, spice levels, Tamil greeting, and seating times that follow the weekday and weekend hours.

Built with

Semantic HTML, one CSS file and vanilla JavaScript. Accessible menus, keyboard and swipe lightbox, and front-end-only forms.
